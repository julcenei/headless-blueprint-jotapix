## Overall Impression

The page peaks in the first second and is weakest at the end — peak-end inverted. "para sua indústria não parar" at 60px, yellow on navy, names the fear precisely; nothing later comes close. At 7s the slide advances mid-paragraph with no way to stop it on a phone. The about+stats block, which exists to reassure, is the emotional low: it wears the visual grammar of social proof and delivers 2001 / 24h / 2 / 8+. Services is the second peak and the strongest content block. Then .cta-band and .site-footer share var(--navy) with no surface change: 1100px of undifferentiated navy at 1440, 1553px at 390 — contradicting DESIGN.md's own "a superfície separa" rule at the one place separation matters most.

The biggest opportunity is not aesthetic. The declared product is the qualified message, and the whole page dispatches the same blank message six times.

## What's Working

1. Typographic discipline that holds the stated tension. Two weights site-wide, an 8-step scale, clamp() display type at -0.025em, section respiro of clamp(3rem, 6.5vw, 6.5rem). The "nossos / produtos" device with a 2px rule reads as editorial layout, not a distributor catalog. Defend this against every future change request.
2. The chamfer is engineered, not decorated. Proportional across five element scales and — the part most implementations get wrong — the focus ring redrawn as a polygon ring inside the shape, because clip-path destroys outline. Pixel-verified: focus survives on a chamfered button.
3. The content buys credibility with specificity. Vulcanização, colagem a frio, guias e taliscas, revestimento de rolos motores, caneca elevadora. With no testimonials, certifications or cases available, technical precision is the only credibility there is.
